## Paper results

The complete battery ran from `2026-09-05T14:04:07+00:00` to `2026-09-05T14:48:59+00:00` (44.88 minutes) on Oracle. The frozen runner and recipe match commit `396d001`; the summarizer is unchanged from `b5bc1b2`. Source hashes match the remote files before and after execution. All eight cases have the same Paper JAR and configuration hashes. [Checksums](benchmark/evidence/oracle-acceptance/SHA256SUMS) cover the copied raw evidence.

The recipe is Paper 26.2 build 121, pinned image digest, seed `20260904`, three CPU cores, 4 GiB heap, 5 GiB container cap without extra swap, view distance 6 and simulation distance 4. ARM64 images and UID/GID 1000 world ownership were checked in the [runtime snapshot](inventory/oracle-runtime-2026-09-05.json). Each bot flew 480 blocks through new chunks, returned through the loaded route, and fought husks for 60 seconds. Paper verified both route endpoints for every bot. Every case saved, stopped, restarted and recovered its block marker.

The unchanged performance rule requires minimum sampled one-minute TPS >=19 and p95 of sampled five-second mean tick time <=50 ms in **both** repetitions. Functional checks passed in all eight cases; the last column applies the performance rule to each case.

Loaded-route and combat phases usually returned to 20 TPS. A percentile of sampled window means is not the p95 of individual ticks: the one-player cases still recorded maximum ticks of 414.8 and 498.2 ms.

New-chunk waits remain inside the phase duration. The eight-player route took 170.1 to 195.7 seconds, compared with 49.3 to 49.5 seconds on the loaded return route; its longest individual waits were 13.9 to 16.2 seconds. ## Network, disks and handoff

The inter-VPS RTT p50/p95 was 194/194 ms in both directions, with no loss in either 100-ping sample. Effective IPv4 MTU was 1500 despite Oracle's 9000-byte interface. Bounded TCP transfers received 38.88 Mbit/s toward Oracle and 49.96 Mbit/s toward Contabo; the reverse transfer recorded 4914 retransmissions. UDP loss toward Contabo was 0.0665%. Read the [network report](benchmark/evidence/network-report.md) for the pacing limits, jitter, exact commands and raw attempts. E1 must configure and verify the tunnel for the measured path.

Both [disk tests](benchmark/evidence/disk-report.md) completed with a temporary 256 MiB file, direct synchronous I/O and one outstanding operation. Their rates include pauses and do not represent saturation throughput. Oracle's sequential write/read rates were 39.22/42.73 MiB/s; random write/read p95 latencies were 0.796/0.737 ms.

At 14:51 UTC, a [fresh SSH check](benchmark/evidence/oracle-after-benchmark-20260905.json) found no running Oracle containers, the Paper container exited with code 0, and no benchmark or internal-port probe listeners. The eight test worlds remain on Oracle for inspection. Temporary measurement ingress was removed from OCI and the host. No private keys, `.env`, world archives or dependency directories were copied into the repository.

Contabo's original E0 Paper server and the Salta application, database and Caddy remained running after the network test; [identity and start-time checks](benchmark/evidence/contabo-after-network-20260905.json) are stored. Before adding E1 control services, stop that retained test Paper container while preserving its world. Earlier [Contabo battery failures](benchmark/evidence/paper-report.md) remain recorded; they qualify no profile and do not establish why the protected app exceeded its latency guard.

E0 proves inventory, prerequisites, measured paths and this bounded Paper profile. K3s installation, its persistence cycles, public player access, R2 backup and worker recovery remain in their later stages. No E1 service was deployed. Repository changes are local on `codex/e0-paper-benchmark`; no push was performed.

## Reproduce verification

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 infra/benchmark/summarize.py --self-test
python3 infra/benchmark/summarize.py infra/benchmark/evidence/oracle-acceptance
cd infra/benchmark/evidence/oracle-acceptance
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
```

To repeat the load test on the prepared, idle Oracle host, use a new label from `/home/ubuntu/cloud-minecraft/benchmark`: `sudo python3 run.py NEW_LABEL --host oracle`. The default is two repetitions of all four player counts. The [README](benchmark/README.md) describes preparation, dependencies, actions and guards.
