# Earlier Oracle Paper results, 2026-09-05

This Paper profile qualified **one player and one instance**. The later [Vanilla test](benchmark/evidence/vanilla-report.md) selected four players and is the current E0 reference.

| Players | Lowest 1-minute TPS, run 1 | Run 2 | Qualified twice? |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 19.5 | 19.5 | Yes |
| 2 | 18.6 | 18.9 | No |
| 4 | 18.4 | 18.4 | No |
| 8 | 18.8 | 19.0 | No |

All eight cases completed actions, saves and marker recovery. The performance rule required at least 19 TPS and p95 sampled five-second mean tick time at most 50 ms in both runs. Every case passed the tick-time requirement.

The first one-minute readings include earlier connection work. Eight passed the second run at 19.0 TPS but failed the first at 18.8. The rule stayed unchanged.

One player completed exploration in 49.3 seconds in each run with no action-phase chunk waits. Even those cases recorded maximum individual ticks of 414.8 and 498.2 ms; a percentile of window averages can hide individual slow ticks.
